## Historical method facts

The bronze ranker used 361 model columns and five context families. The archived
model contained:

| family | trees | training queries | validation queries | validation top-k | validation top-1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| main | 346 | 348,951 | 38,773 | 0.6815 | 0.6815 |
| c7 | 360 | 108,342 | 11,925 | 0.7381 | 0.7315 |
| mid | 237 | 262,831 | 29,203 | 0.8843 | 0.8918 |
| low | 54 | 23,141 | 2,450 | 0.7927 | 0.8431 |
| easy | 170 | 39,593 | 4,372 | 0.9090 | 0.8995 |

The historical training window was July 28–August 8, 2026 UTC. The archived
report recorded 13,571 winning exact-deck teacher seats, 869,846 decision
queries, a 900-round LightGBM LambdaRank ceiling, seed 0, and family-specific
leaf limits. These are historical measurements and are not regenerated by this
release.

## What is retained

The public tree retains generic numeric-row validation, grouped ranking
training, LightGBM tree flattening, pure scoring, stable top-k selection, parity
checking, configuration examples, tests, and documentation. The competition
observation adapter, card metadata, deck, replay reader, teacher index, data
files, official runtime, and trained model are not retained.

## Why the artifacts are excluded

The official rules limit Competition Data to competition participation and
require deletion after the competition. They also state that Pokémon Elements
and related materials remain with their owners, and specifically restrict use
of models trained on Pokémon Elements outside participation in the Competition.
The local engine README/license likewise describes the engine as competition-use
only and not open-source. Because no clear post-competition redistribution
permission is available for the historical model or deck, this release fails
closed and excludes them. See `docs/license_audit.md` for the file-level
classification and citations to the governing sources.
